## Scope reconciliation

The original MVP requires lecturer assignment/progress visibility and author submission/review, plus limited reviewed Core/Future modules and simulations. Those are not silently removed by the narrower tested pilot. Author CRUD is explicitly deferred in the accepted continuation, but finishing the original MVP requires implementing it or an explicit revised scope decision. Missing lecturer/project UI is classified MISSING at the workflow level even though schema and contracts exist. AI Mentor remains separately deferred.

No claim is made that source records marked checked establish all lessons' human approval. The static v0.4.0 pilot records refer to a prior independent staging approval; Stage 1 inspected their presence and gating, not the reviewer's identity, qualifications or private evidence.
