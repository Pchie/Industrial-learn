## Selection Decision

OpenStax is the current principal academic foundation because its exact section is
lawfully accessible and directly supports the introductory relationship. Penn State
independently corroborates pressure as normal force per unit area and the SI unit
relationship. This combination remains below the preferred Level 3 textbook benchmark,
so academic source quality is `PARTIAL`, not fully approved.

No commercial textbook chapter, diagram, worked example, or PDF is stored or reproduced
by this register.
