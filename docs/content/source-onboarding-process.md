# Source Onboarding Process

## Purpose

Industrial Learn source onboarding prevents unverified engineering claims from entering student-facing content.

Source IDs: IL-AGENTS-001, IL-CONTENT-GOV-001, IL-ACADEMIC-SOURCE-POLICY-001.

## Steps

1. Inspect `sources/` for actual files before editing content.
2. Classify the source using the policy's Level 1-5 authority hierarchy and its role in the evidence package.
3. For academic theory, check whether an appropriate, legally available McGraw Hill engineering title exists. If an alternative is selected, record why it is academically equivalent or why the preferred source was unavailable or insufficient.
4. Confirm title, author or organisation, publisher when applicable, edition or version, publication date, access date, document type, copyright status, project access and file integrity.
5. Assign or reuse a stable `SRC-*` ID.
6. Record relevant chapters, sections and pages only when they are visible in the actual source metadata.
7. Record the knowledge files, equations and simulations supported by the source.
8. Check for conflicts with other credible sources and document any disagreement, assumptions and applicable engineering regime.
9. Corroborate significant equations, simulations, design principles, safety limits, fault behaviour and operating limits with a second credible source where practical.
10. Convert reviewed sections into focused knowledge files using original explanations and visuals.
11. Keep every content, equation and simulation record at `Source required` until review evidence exists.
12. Submit content to the review workflow without self-approval.

## Missing Source Rule

When the source file is absent, record a source-needed item and do not invent citation details, page ranges, clauses, ratings or source approval.

## Copyright Rule

Do not store full copyrighted standards or textbook chapters unless the project has a lawful licence and storage approval. Public web access is not redistribution permission. Knowledge files must use original explanations, short compliant quotations only where essential, original visuals and examples, and precise references.

## Preferred Source Acquisition

An official McGraw Hill product listing can establish that a relevant title exists, but it does not
establish that Industrial Learn has lawful access to its content. Record a title as an acquisition
candidate until the exact edition and project licence or purchase are documented. Do not cite unseen
chapter content, page ranges, examples, figures, or equations.

## Source Conflict Rule

Do not silently select between conflicting credible sources. Record the disagreement and set the
affected content to `Engineering review required` until a reviewer resolves the applicable regime.
