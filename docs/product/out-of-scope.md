# Industrial Learn First-Release Out Of Scope

## Purpose

This file protects the first release from becoming too large for a small development team. Exclusions can be revisited after the MVP proves the learning model, content review model, calculation discipline, and lecturer workflow.

## Excluded From First Release

### Broad Curriculum Coverage

- Full engineering degree coverage.
- All disciplines and all year levels.
- Complete exam preparation banks.
- Region-specific curriculum variants for multiple institutions.

### Advanced AI Features

- Open-ended AI tutoring.
- AI-generated engineering answers without review.
- Automated approval of technical content.
- AI grading of high-stakes submissions.
- AI retrieval features that bypass approved source IDs.

### High-Stakes Credentialing

- Professional certification.
- Licence preparation claims.
- Formal accreditation claims.
- High-stakes proctored exams.

### Complex Institution Administration

- Multi-campus administration.
- Advanced role hierarchy.
- Billing, procurement, and subscription management.
- Deep learning management system integrations.
- Automated timetable integration.

### Live Equipment And Industrial Integration

- Live equipment control.
- Real plant, machine, or laboratory system integration.
- Remote switching, actuation, or safety-critical monitoring.
- Manufacturer-specific equipment rating libraries without reviewed source records.

### Advanced Simulation Platform

- Large-scale 3D simulation environments.
- Real-time multiplayer labs.
- Complex finite element, computational fluid dynamics, or power-system analysis engines.
- User-authored simulation scripting.

### Full Authoring Suite

- Rich drag-and-drop lesson builder.
- Full media production workflow.
- Advanced version branching and merging.
- Marketplace for community content.

### Advanced Analytics

- Predictive risk scoring.
- Institution-wide benchmarking.
- Longitudinal graduate outcome analytics.
- Automated intervention recommendations.

### Native Mobile Apps

- Native iOS app.
- Native Android app.
- Offline-first native app sync.

The first release should be responsive web-first.

### Payment And Commerce

- Student subscriptions.
- Institutional billing.
- Marketplace purchases.
- Paid certificates.

### Public Community Features

- Forums.
- Public profiles.
- Peer-to-peer messaging.
- Open project galleries.
- Social posting.

## Explicit Non-Goals

- Do not replace lecturers.
- Do not replace laboratory safety instruction.
- Do not present simulations as real equipment certification.
- Do not make unsupported claims about standards, clauses, ratings, or manufacturer data.
- Do not label any content approved without a review record.
- Do not treat Future Engineering content as speculative trend writing; it must remain grounded in reviewed technical sources.
