# Content Review Workflow

## Purpose

Industrial Learn content must be reviewed before student publication. Draft labels must not be hidden to make unreviewed content appear approved.

Source IDs: IL-AGENTS-001, IL-PRD-001, IL-DAL-001.

## Content-Type Requirements

| Content type                     | Required review evidence                                                        |
| -------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Theory lesson                    | Sources, educational structure, engineering approval                            |
| Calculation lesson               | Sources, equation review, safety review, engineering approval                   |
| Simulation lesson                | Sources, equation review, simulation tests, safety review, engineering approval |
| Assessment                       | Sources, equation review where applicable, engineering approval                 |
| Engineering project              | Sources, safety review, engineering approval                                    |
| Professional-development content | Educational structure review                                                    |

## Role Rules

- Content authors create drafts, edit draft versions, attach sources, respond to comments, and submit for review.
- Lecturers review educational structure where authorised but do not automatically approve engineering accuracy.
- Engineering reviewers check sources, equations, safety content, simulation evidence, and engineering approval.
- Administrators publish, roll back, archive, and manage governance operations without silently bypassing required evidence.

## Self-Approval

Authors cannot approve their own technical content by default. Any exception must be explicit policy and audit-recorded.

## Rollback

Rollback selects a previously published version, preserves the withdrawn version, records actor and reason, and does not alter historical assessment attempt references.
