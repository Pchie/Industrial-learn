# Industrial Learn User Personas

## Persona 1: First-Year Engineering Student

Name: Thabo

Role: First-semester engineering student

Goals:

- Understand foundational concepts without feeling lost.
- Learn how to approach calculations step by step.
- Build confidence before tutorials, labs, and tests.

Pain points:

- Finds lectures fast and abstract.
- Makes unit mistakes.
- Does not always know which formula applies.
- Needs practice outside scheduled class time.

Industrial Learn must help by:

- Explaining concepts in plain, accurate language.
- Showing calculation steps with assumptions and units.
- Providing immediate feedback on common mistakes.
- Connecting fundamentals to practical engineering examples.

## Persona 2: Applied Engineering Student

Name: Aisha

Role: Diploma or vocational engineering student

Goals:

- Connect theory to equipment, faults, measurements, and practical decisions.
- Practise safely before physical lab work.
- Build a portfolio of engineering projects.

Pain points:

- Textbook explanations can feel detached from real systems.
- Lab access is limited.
- Fault diagnosis is hard to practise repeatedly.
- Wants clear links between calculations and practical outcomes.

Industrial Learn must help by:

- Providing simulations with normal, boundary, and fault states.
- Offering diagnostic exercises.
- Linking calculations to observable system behaviour.
- Supporting project-based evidence of learning.

## Persona 3: Final-Year Student

Name: Daniel

Role: Senior undergraduate preparing for work

Goals:

- Revise fundamentals quickly.
- Practise design judgement and assumptions.
- Prepare for junior professional expectations.
- Explore future engineering technologies.

Pain points:

- Has gaps in earlier topics.
- Needs to justify decisions, not only produce answers.
- Wants future technology content that is credible rather than hype-driven.

Industrial Learn must help by:

- Offering targeted revision and progress tracking.
- Providing design exercises with constraints and trade-offs.
- Connecting Core Engineering to Future Engineering.
- Requiring sourced technical claims and reviewed content.

## Persona 4: Junior Engineer

Name: Priya

Role: Junior engineer in early professional practice

Goals:

- Refresh engineering fundamentals.
- Practise structured fault diagnosis.
- Improve technical communication.
- Understand modern tools and technologies in context.

Pain points:

- Needs fast but trustworthy revision.
- Cannot rely on unsourced internet answers.
- Must learn to explain limitations and assumptions clearly.

Industrial Learn must help by:

- Providing source-backed content.
- Showing calculations transparently.
- Offering professional reflection prompts.
- Separating reviewed knowledge from draft or source-required material.

## Persona 5: Lecturer

Name: Dr. Maseko

Role: Engineering lecturer

Goals:

- Assign focused learning activities.
- Identify student misconceptions.
- Reduce repeated remedial explanation.
- Use content that has credible technical review.

Pain points:

- Student progress data is often too broad.
- Preparing simulations and fault cases takes time.
- Students arrive with uneven background knowledge.
- Trust is difficult when digital content is not reviewed.

Industrial Learn must help by:

- Providing assignable modules and assessments.
- Showing progress by concept, calculation, simulation, and project.
- Making content status and review state visible.
- Supporting small, reliable topic coverage before expanding.

## Persona 6: Content Author

Name: Lerato

Role: Engineering educator or instructional designer

Goals:

- Create clear lessons, assessments, projects, and simulations.
- Track what still needs sourcing or engineering review.
- Avoid mixing formulas, UI wording, and review metadata.

Pain points:

- Content can become inconsistent across topics.
- Review feedback can be difficult to track.
- It is easy for drafts to look final before they are reviewed.

Industrial Learn must help by:

- Enforcing content statuses.
- Requiring source IDs for important technical statements.
- Separating content, calculations, simulations, and review records.
- Showing what is blocked before approval.

## Persona 7: Engineering Reviewer

Name: Johan

Role: Practising engineer or senior academic reviewer

Goals:

- Confirm technical correctness.
- Check source references, formulas, units, assumptions, and simulation behaviour.
- Prevent unsafe or unsupported claims from reaching students.

Pain points:

- Reviews are slow when sources and equations are scattered.
- It is hard to see what changed between versions.
- Simulations require clear expected behaviour to review properly.

Industrial Learn must help by:

- Presenting source IDs, assumptions, equations, and test expectations clearly.
- Keeping review records connected to content status.
- Requiring correction before approval.
- Supporting normal-state, boundary-state, and fault-state review.
