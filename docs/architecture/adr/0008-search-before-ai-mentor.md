# ADR 0008: Search Before AI Mentor

Status: Proposed

## Context

The product includes a future AI mentor direction, but the MVP excludes broad AI tutoring and automated engineering judgement. The platform first needs reviewed knowledge, source IDs, and content governance. Sources: IL-PRD-001, IL-MVP-001.

## Decision

Build search and reviewed retrieval foundations before introducing an AI mentor. The AI mentor remains a future boundary until source governance, review coverage, and retrieval filtering are reliable.

## Consequences

- MVP risk is lower because core learning and review workflows come first.
- Future AI services can rely on reviewed knowledge files and source IDs.
- AI cannot become an unreviewed shortcut around engineering review.
