## Your Industrial Learn Pilot

This is a small, voluntary test of one engineering learning experience. It is not a
finished course, an exam or a professional engineering service. Your participation and
feedback will not affect university grades, and you may stop without giving a reason.

Dates: `[pilot start/end and timezone]`. Your session: `[booked session]`.
Support: `[support contact and staffed hours]`. Use the pilot only during agreed sessions.

You will use Basic Fluid Pressure, its visual pressure activity and a short practice
assessment. No standalone simulation is included yet; an empty Simulation Lab is expected.
Do not use the platform to set up equipment, make safety decisions or approve a design.
Ask your lecturer or a qualified engineer for real engineering decisions.

## Getting Started

1. Follow the individual invitation and `[tested private staging access instructions]`.
   The site is the Industrial Learn staging site, not production. Do not forward access
   links or ask for access to the team's hosting or database account.
2. Choose **Sign up**, use your own email address and a unique password, and follow the
   verification message. Never send us your password, code or verification/recovery link.
3. If verification does not arrive, check spam, then contact support during staffed hours.
   Do not repeatedly create accounts, use someone else's account or disable security settings.
4. Sign in and open **Workspace**, then the pilot learning collection at `/learn/pilot`.
   Your account should have Student access. Report unexpected staff tools or another
   person's information immediately; do not explore it.
5. Spend a few minutes with the facilitator on navigation, how to stop and how to get help.
   Tell us any device or navigation adjustment you need without disclosing medical details.
6. Open **Basic Fluid Pressure**. Try its controls, read the measurements and explanations,
   and complete the challenge. Take as much time as you need; optional detail is available.
7. Skip standalone simulations for this pilot. Continue to the short assessment, submit
   once, then review the feedback. We want to understand the experience, not rank students.
8. Check your saved progress, complete the optional `[private feedback link]`, and sign out
   when finished, especially on a shared device.

Opening a page does not mean you have completed it. A saved/completed status records an
activity, not professional competence or a passing university mark. Ask support if a
submission looks stuck; do not keep clicking Submit.

## Tell Us What Went Wrong

Use `[support contact]` for a bug, confusing explanation, account issue, accessibility
problem or possible engineering error. Include your pilot alias, the page/section,
approximate time, expected behaviour and what happened. A short description is enough.
Do not include private answers, other people's details, browser logs, passwords or tokens.
Only share a screenshot after removing personal details and assessment answers.

If an engineering result appears wrong, you see someone else's information, answers appear
before submission, or the core activity is inaccessible, **stop and tell the facilitator
immediately**. Do not test the problem further or share it in a public group.

## Your Data and Choices

The platform uses your account details and private learning activity to provide the lesson,
assessment feedback and progress. With your permission, the pilot team takes brief notes
and collects optional feedback under a pilot alias. There is no session recording by default.
We do not ask for student numbers, transcripts or sensitive university records.

Authorised staff use this information to find problems and improve the experience.
Feedback summaries will not name you; staff access is limited by role. Staging hosting and
authentication use Vercel and Supabase. The privacy contact will explain access and retention
before you agree: `[approved retention dates and exceptions]`. We cannot promise instant
erasure from security audit history or backups.

You can skip a question, decline observation notes or stop participating. Contact
`[privacy contact]` about access to your data, withdrawal or deletion. If observation is
declined, the platform still needs its normal account/progress data to function; the
facilitator will explain this separately. No public certificate or public score is issued.
