## Risk Acceptance Rules

- R01-R05 cannot be accepted silently to change this verdict. Record closure evidence
  and obtain a new readiness decision before inviting anyone.
- Manual monitoring and feedback may replace new infrastructure for this small,
  supervised cohort only after G05 is satisfied. That is not permission for unattended use.
- Treat inherited synthetic tests as regression evidence, not proof of delivery to a
  real mailbox or success for a student with assistive technology.
- Retain the six fixtures and historical approvals as audit evidence until an authorised
  remediation determines safe quarantine/retention. This report does not authorise deletion.
- Reassess all open risks after any code, configuration, publication, policy, or hosting
  protection change. A pilot exit decision does not authorise production or AI Mentor.
